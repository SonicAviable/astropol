SmartBiz, the Russian-Kazakh game studio behind Astropolity, is looking for people who want gamedev experience and a portfolio built on a real project. The role is unpaid and open to beginners.
# WHO WE NEED
- Designers — interface, icons, promo art.
- 3D artists — ships, stations, planets.
- SMM managers — posts, community, promotion.
- News editors — update news and announcements.
- Testers — finding bugs, checking balance.
# WHAT YOU GET
- Work on a released game: Astropolity is on sale on VK Play and among its top sellers.
- A project for your portfolio and a place in the credits.
- A part in preparing the Steam release at the end of 2026.
- Remote work and tasks that fit your level and free time.
# HOW TO APPLY
Message our game community: tell us the role, a bit about yourself and attach your work if you have any.
