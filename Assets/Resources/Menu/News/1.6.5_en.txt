Your empire gets an advisor, every game its own chronicle with charts, and your ruler new goals for the years ahead.
# ORACLE-7 ADVISOR
- Tells you when construction or science ships sit idle, helium-3 goes negative or a research slot is empty.
- Warns about enemies in your systems, looming unrest and a fleet over capacity.
- Lets you know when an assault is possible or a new colony is within reach. Click a tip to jump to the system.
- Can be turned off in Settings: "Advisor" → "In-game advice".
# GAME SUMMARY
- Open it from the pause menu or the victory/defeat screen.
- Yearly charts for every empire: score, systems, population, technologies, fleet power.
- 12 stat tiles and a chronicle of the game: contacts, wars, captures, assaults, achievements. Click an entry to move the camera there.
# RANDOM EMPIRE GOALS
- After the starter goals (or 10 years in) your empire offers three goals to choose from.
- Each goal has 10 years. Success pays out alloys, titanium, helium-3 or influence.
- The current goal is shown in the bottom-right corner; double-click the cross to abandon it.
# NEW SOUNDS
- 22 interface, notification, jump, weapon and explosion sounds replaced with studio recordings.
# INTERFACE
- New main menu: continue your last game in one click (or press Enter) and read the news right in the game.
- Reworked pause menu: empire summary, two-column buttons, a separate exit block.
- The event log opens on the left. System labels on the map no longer overlap.
# FIXES
- The game should no longer crash on launch from VK Play in windowed mode on laptops with two GPUs.
