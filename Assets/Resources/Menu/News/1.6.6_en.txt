The colony and research screens are rebuilt, covert operations get their own window, and you can now report bugs right from the game.
# COLONY SCREEN
- The planet sits in the center with callouts around it: habitability, society and stability, deposits.
- Districts are cards fanned out below the planet: output, workers in their slots, growth. Click a card to prioritize that resource; drag workers between cards.
- The fifth card, "+ Build", opens the build menu. Planet projects are listed on the right.
# RESEARCH
- New window: science branches on the left, the selected branch's tree with tiers and eras, a detailed tech card on the right.
- Research slots at the bottom: progress, ETA, pause and cancel.
- Technologies are getting their own icons instead of shared category icons.
# COVERT OPERATIONS
- Agents shown as portrait columns: home counter-intelligence, hiring candidates, operations log and intel on empires.
# COLONY MANAGEMENT
- Empire summary, a "Needs attention" list, colony filters and sorting, one-click auto-build everywhere.
# FEEDBACK
- Pause menu → "Feedback": bug, idea or other. Optionally attach system info and the game log.
# INTERFACE
- Windows are back to translucent "liquid glass"; many have live backdrops with nebulae and shooting stars.
- First-launch screen: the game picks graphics quality for your PC, and you choose language, screen mode, UI scale, volume and your experience — it sets the tutorial, advisor and first-game difficulty.
