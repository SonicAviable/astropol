The galaxy map comes alive: 3D stars and planets, flowing hyperlanes and ten new kinds of stars. The interface is rebuilt from the top bar to the event window.
# GALAXY
- Up close, systems show real 3D stars and photo-textured planets on elliptical orbits with fading trails.
- "Hyperflow" lanes: gentle arcs from system edge to system edge, energy flowing along them, portals at both ends. Your lanes are teal, your neighbours' take their empire colour.
- A roomier galaxy with nebulae and dust lanes for a backdrop; zooming out no longer fades the map to black.
- A cinematic look: glowing stars, lens flares, softer planet night sides. Stars hum with plasma as you approach.
# NEW STARS
- 10 new types: blue supergiant, red giant, white and brown dwarfs, binary star, Wolf-Rayet, pulsar, magnetar, protostar, planetary nebula.
- The star shapes the number of planets, world types and deposits. Its effect is shown in the system tooltip.
- Natural wonders, 3 to 8 per galaxy: a magnetar disables shields, an ion storm eats hulls, the Shroud hides fleets, stellar nurseries boost population growth.
# NEW HUD
- Top bar: big resource numbers, a date disc with the real Moon phase and a speed scale, the ruler's portrait with a stability ring.
- Resource tooltips: stock and monthly income, income sources and expenses with each one's share, a monthly total and storage fill.
- "Broadcast" galactic news ticker at the bottom, hex menu at the side, a minimap with a scale, and the planner.
- Work in a system is a ring around the star and a beacon with days left, so you see when an outpost or colony is ready.
# WINDOWS
- "Cinema" events: full-width artwork, answer options as cards with their consequences.
- New empire selection: faction crests, a comm link with the ruler and a dossier.
- New "Orbit" loading screen: full-screen artwork, a progress ring with percent, loading stages and tips.
- "Medallion" empire overview and new interface sounds.
# CARD BATTLE
- Faction doctrines, a once-per-battle hero order, turn-5 battle events and a win chance forecast for every sector.
- Hit-stop on impacts, slow motion on big kills and kill streaks: "Double strike", "Rout", "Slaughter".
# FIXES
- Space and Esc no longer open windows on their own. Clicking the minimap moves the camera exactly there.
- The camera reaches the edge of large galaxies; science ships no longer stick to an old target.
- No more lag on Retina Macs. Asteroids no longer vanish in builds.
