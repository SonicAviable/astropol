Good news for Mac owners: we are preparing a macOS version of Astropolity.
# WHAT THE MAC VERSION INCLUDES
- The same game as on Windows: the galaxy, six empires, diplomacy, fleets and card assaults on colonies.
- Updates will ship on both systems at the same time.
- Support for Macs with Apple silicon (M1 and newer).
# WHEN
We will announce the release date once testing is done. Follow the community news.
# HELP US TEST
If you have a Mac and want to try the game before release, message our community.
