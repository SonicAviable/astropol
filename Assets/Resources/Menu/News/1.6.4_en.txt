Real-time ground battles are gone — colonies are now taken with card assaults. The army is built on a separate military budget, and the whole interface moved to dark navy tones.
# CARD ASSAULTS
- Besiege a system, suppress its starbase, and the system panel shows an "ASSAULT" button.
- The battle is fought over three planet sectors for 8 turns: hold two sectors or break into the HQ.
- 49 cards: infantry, vehicles, orders, structures and traps, plus 4 unique cards per faction.
- Surviving cards become veterans, wounded ones heal for 30 days. There is an "AUTO BATTLE" button.
# ARSENAL AND MILITARY BUDGET
- The Arsenal (J) is your empire's army. Set its makeup or switch on "Auto".
- The army is funded from a share of monthly income and no longer drains stockpiles needed for building and fleets.
- The assault forecast shows the win chance over 40 auto battles.
# FORTIFICATIONS
- Fortress level is shown right on the map; starbases have their own window.
- Each fortification level gives the defence a turret and an extra card slot.
