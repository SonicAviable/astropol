## Чудеса фракций (5 штук)

### 1. Wonder_Dyson — «Сфера Дайсона» (Синдикат Аквила, золото)
```
A colossal Dyson sphere under construction around a blazing yellow-white star: a geodesic shell of thousands of triangular dark solar-collector panels framed in glowing gold, the equatorial belt finished, the upper and lower parts still an open golden lattice, brilliant jets of harvested energy streaming out of both poles; tiny freighters and construction drones swarm around it for scale; feeling of immense corporate wealth and power; warm gold and amber against deep blue space
```

### 2. Wonder_Ring — «Кольцо Согласия» (Республика Астрея, бирюза)
```
A vast orbital ring habitat encircling a sun, seen at a dramatic tilted angle: the inner surface is a continuous ribbon of green continents, blue seas, white clouds and glittering city lights, bordered by tall silver retaining walls with cyan edge lights; three massive anchor stations sit on the ring; the star glows in the center; elegant, hopeful, utopian atmosphere; cyan, white and soft gold palette
```

### 3. Wonder_Gate — «Врата Провидения» (Иридийский Оракул, фиолетовый)
```
An enormous crystalline star gate floating in deep space: a ring of twelve violet crystal segments with sharp spires and four great crystal pylons, the inside of the ring filled with a swirling luminous vortex of violet and white light that folds space like fabric; a small fleet of elegant organic-looking ships glides through it; mystical, prophetic, otherworldly mood; violet, magenta and silver palette, distant purple nebula
```

### 4. Wonder_Forge — «Наковальня Ксарна» (Доминион Ксарн, красный)
```
A titanic stellar forge around an orange-red star: three heavy gyroscopic rings made of dark armored steel blocks with red-hot glowing seams rotate around the star at different angles, ripping streams of plasma from the corona and channeling them into a massive anvil-shaped foundry station the size of a moon, molten metal glowing inside it; warships under construction nearby; brutal industrial militaristic grandeur; black steel, ember orange and crimson palette
```

### 5. Wonder_Tree — «Древо Миров» (Конклав Тэрра'ан, зелёный)
```
A colossal living world-tree megastructure growing through space: three gigantic bark-covered trunks rooted in an asteroid belt spiral toward a golden star and branch into a vast cradle-like crown that embraces the star, glowing green-gold sap veins, bioluminescent seed pods and leaves made of light; drifting spores sparkle in the starlight; ancient, sacred, serene atmosphere; deep greens, emerald and gold palette
```

## Цепочки событий (7 штук)

### 6. Heart — «Сердце Тишины» (находка, пробуждение, ответ)
```
A perfectly black geometric polyhedron the size of a shuttle lies in an icy crevasse on a frozen moon, absorbing all light; frost forms in concentric rings around it; tiny astronauts with helmet lamps stand at the edge, their light beams vanishing into the object; a pale gas giant hangs in the black sky; eerie silent atmosphere; cold blue and black palette with a faint violet glow
```

### 7. HeartVisitors — «Посланники Строителей»
```
A ship made of frozen liquid light, shaped like a suspended teardrop, hovers silently above a futuristic capital city at night; crowds on rooftops and terraces look up in awe; soft beams of white light descend onto a central plaza; mystery and reverence; white, pale gold and deep blue palette
```

### 8. Ark — «Ковчег „Надежда“» (сигнал, капитан)
```
An ancient sublight generation ship drifting through interstellar darkness: a kilometers-long cylindrical vessel with rotating habitat drums, patched hull plates, faded markings and ice crusted on its bow, a dim nuclear engine glow at the stern; a sleek modern tug ship approaches with searchlights; melancholy and hope; muted steel, rust and warm amber palette
```

### 9. ArkDawn — «Рассвет» (прибытие, потомки)
```
Thousands of colonists stepping out of old landing shuttles onto a green planet at sunrise, seeing the open sky for the first time in their lives; an elderly woman captain in a worn uniform kneels and touches the soil; crowds of welcoming citizens with banners and lights; emotional, hopeful, golden sunrise light
```

### 10. Mind — «Пробуждённый разум»
```
A planetary control center at night where a vast artificial intelligence becomes self-aware: a towering holographic neural lattice glows cyan and white above rows of consoles, its light forming the calm outline of an abstract face; engineers stare up in awe; city lights and reactor towers visible through panoramic windows; tense, philosophical mood; cyan, teal and deep navy palette
```

### 11. Brood — «Кладка Пустоты» (яйца, детёныши)
```
A clutch of enormous translucent eggs frozen in the ice of a gas giant's ring, each the size of a habitat module, with faint glowing hearts beating inside; a small science ship's spotlights illuminate them; the striped gas giant and its rings fill the background; wonder and danger; icy blue, teal and soft bioluminescent pink palette
```

### 12. BroodGrown — «Стая Пустоты» (выросшие левиафаны, мать)
```
A herd of majestic space leviathans — translucent whale-like creatures with bioluminescent patterns and long flowing fins — swimming through space alongside a small fleet of warships in formation near a blue star; sense of scale, majesty and tamed wildness; teal, violet and starlight palette
```
